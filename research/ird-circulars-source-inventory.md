## Scope and retrieval notes

This is an index of the **67** document links currently exposed by the official IRD Circulars page. The page presents one unpaginated, newest-first list (06 August 2026 through 05 August 2008); no year/category filters or next-page controls were visible. All listed items link to PDFs under the official IRD `Circulars_Circulars` path.

The index groups all material under the single heading “List of Circulars”; it does **not** provide formal categories. For the future individual-tax corpus, the practical topic groups are:

- Individual income / instalments: 3 current computation or instalment entries (2026/E/06 revised, 2026/E/06, 2025/E/08), plus 2023/E/03.
- Employment / APIT: 13 entries, including the 2026 salary-arrears circular, APIT employer guidance, and non-cash-benefit circulars.

- Other (VAT, SVAT, stamp duty, levies, sector-specific matters): 27 entries.

### Important interpretation constraints

- Retain revised and original documents together. The index contains apparent original/revised pairs for 2026/E/06, 2022/E/06, 2022/E/05, and 2020/E/03; it also lists a 2025 update numbered 2023/E/06-1 alongside 2023/E/06. A later circular may supersede an earlier one, but that relationship should be verified from the document text before a calculator relies on it.
- The index itself displays “Last updated: 11-04-2023”, despite listing 2025–2026 material. Treat the document dates and links as the usable evidence, and re-check the index before each source refresh.
- The two May 2019 stamp-duty entries have conflicting-looking numbering in their titles/filenames (one link is `SEC_2019_02_E.pdf`, the next `SEC_2019_01_E.pdf`). Preserve the official links rather than normalising this metadata.
- This inventory records the index as published; it does not claim every historical circular remains legally operative. The future filing/calculation rules must be validated against the current Act, amendments, gazettes, and applicable tax-year materials.
