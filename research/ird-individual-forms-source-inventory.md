# IRD individual-tax forms: official source inventory

Retrieved: 13 August 2026 (Asia/Colombo)
Official source index: <https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/Downloads/SitePages/Forms.aspx?menuid=1603>

## Scope and current-year result

This inventory covers the currently published official IRD documents an **individual** may need to complete for an income-tax filing, tax registration/update, APIT/WHT declaration, estimate, refund, or a closely related individual request. It excludes employer annual statements, partnership/company returns, VAT, and other entity-only material.

The IRD Forms and Returns index (last updated **09 July 2026**) publishes an `Individuals` return set for **Y/A 2025/2026**. At retrieval, it does **not** show a `Returns of Income - Year of Assessment 2026/2027` section or a 2026/2027 individual return/schedule/asset-statement form. Accordingly, do not reuse the 2025/2026 return for 2026/2027 without checking the official index again; the 2026/2027 instalment rules are a separate circular, not a return form.

The return itself says electronic filing is mandatory and gives 30 November 2026 as the submission deadline for Y/A 2025/2026. The linked PDFs below are the official paper/reference forms and guidance, not confirmation that a paper submission is accepted.

## Exclusions and maintenance rule

- The Forms page's APIT annual statement and WHT/AIT annual/quarterly statements are preparer/employer/withholding-agent returns, so they are excluded from the individual taxpayer's filing pack.
- VAT, CGT standalone returns, stamp duty, partnerships, companies, entities, and corporations are excluded. A capital gain may still be reported through the individual return schedules; use a standalone CGT return only after confirming that it applies to the transaction and taxpayer.
- Before downloading or building calculations for **Y/A 2026/2027**, refresh the official Forms index and add the new individual return set only once IRD publishes it. Keep this inventory separate from the 2026/2027 instalment circulars already catalogued under `research/ird-circulars-source-inventory.md`.
