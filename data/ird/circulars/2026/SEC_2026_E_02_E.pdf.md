Circular No. SEC/2026/E./ OR

Specific Instructions Applicable to Registered Persons Making Supplies on a Non-
Foreign Exchange (NFE) Basis to another registered person who manufactures

goods for export

Under Non-Foreign Exchange (NFE) transactions, an overseas buyer places orders with a local
manufacturer for export purposes on the basis that the required raw materials are supplied to the
manufacturer without cost. In such instances, the raw materials remain the property of the overseas
buyer and are provided solely for conversion into finished goods, which are subsequently exported
to the same buyer.

In certain cases, an overseas buyer may place export orders with a local goods manufacturer under
NFE basis where the required raw materials are procured from a local raw materials supplier. At
the request of the overseas buyer, such raw materials are delivered directly by the local supplier to
the manufacturer for the purpose of export production. Payment for these raw materials is made
directly by the overseas buyer to the local supplier in foreign currency.

In this context, the supply of raw materials by the local supplier to the manufacturer is treated as a
taxable supply for the purposes of Value Added Tax (VAT), in accordance with the provisions of
the Value Added Tax Act, No. 14 of 2002 (as amended). Previously, such transactions were
facilitated under the Simplified Value Added Tax (SVAT) Scheme, whereby both the NFE
manufacturer and the NFE supplier operated under designated categories, supported by SVAT
credit vouchers to account for the VAT applicable on such supplies.

However, following the repeal of the SVAT Scheme, there is no explicit framework governing the
treatment of NFE transactions, resulting in practical challenges in their implementation.

Accordingly, the following guidelines are issued to establish a clear and consistent procedure for
managing supplies conducted under NFE basis.

These instructions apply where a registered person supplies goods on Non-Foreign Exchange
(NFE) basis or on non-cash basis, on behalf of a foreign purchaser to another registered person
who manufactures goods for export.

For the purposes of these instructions:

(i). The registered person supplying such goods shall hereinafter be referred to as the “NFE
Supplier”; and

(ii). The registered person who manufactures the goods for export shall hereinafter be referred
to as the “NFE Manufacturer.”

The NFE Supplier shall issue a Tax Invoice in accordance with section 20 of the Value Added Tax
Act, No. 14 of 2002, as amended (hereinafter referred to as the “VAT Act”), in respect of supplies
made on NFE or non-cash basis, subject to strict compliance with the following conditions:

(a) The NFE Supplier shall obtain a written delivery request from the NFE manufacturer for the
delivery of such goods. Such delivery request shall mandatorily include:

i A description of the goods to be purchased;

ii The quantity of goods; and

iii The names and addresses of the foreign purchaser to whom the finished products are
intended to be exported.

iv Purchase order reference number and date received to the NFE supplier from the foreign
purchaser for the raw materials.

(b) The NFE Supplier shall possess a valid purchase order issued by the foreign purchaser,
containing instructions to deliver the goods directly to the NFE manufacturer. The name and
address of the issuer of such purchase order, together with the description and quantity of
goods, shall be identical to the details stated in the delivery request referred to in paragraph (a)
above.

(c) Settlement for such supplies shall be made exclusively through foreign currency received
directly from the foreign purchaser through a bank in Sri Lanka licensed under the Banking
Act, No. 30 of 1988.

(d) No settlement shall be made from any other source. Any adjustment, set-off, or settlement of
a third-party liability of the foreign purchaser, whether effected with or without the foreign
purchaser’s consent, shall not be permitted.

(e) The NFE Manufacturer shall export the finished goods solely to the same foreign purchaser
referred to in the relevant delivery request and purchase order. The finished goods shall not be
exported to any third party, whether with or without the consent of the foreign purchaser.

(f) The NFE Manufacturer shall pay the Value Added Tax (VAT) amount relating to the NFE
supply which is specified in the Tax Invoice to the NFE Supplier.

(g) Subject to compliance with the provisions of Section 22 of the VAT Act, the NFE
Manufacturer may claim the VAT paid as input tax against its output tax. However, the value
of such purchases shall not be deducted as an expense by the NFE Manufacturer when
determining its profit or loss for the income tax purpose.

(h) The NFE Manufacturer shall declare the local value addition, commonly referred to as “Cut
and Make Charges” in VAT Schedule 06, even though the export documentation reflects the
national FOB value, which includes both the cost of raw materials and local value addition.
However, the NFE Manufacturer shall provide a reconciliation for any discrepancies in export
documentation and foreign currency remittances.

(i) The NFE Manufacturer shall declare all tax invoices issued on an NFE basis in VAT Schedule
02, including the term “NFE” in the description of the goods.

(j) The NFE Supplier shall declare all tax invoices issued on an NFE basis in VAT Schedule 01,
including the term “NFE” in the description of the goods.

(k) Both the NFE Supplier and NFE Manufacturer shall maintain all supporting documents
substantiating the information declared in the relevant VAT forms and shall produce such
documents when required by the Commissioner-General or any authorized officer.
