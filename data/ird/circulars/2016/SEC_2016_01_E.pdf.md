              Circular to Banks, Financial Institutions and Importers of Motor Vehicle



              Imposition of Vehicle Entitlement Levy (VEL) as proposed in the Budget
              2016

              As instructed by the Ministry of Finance on implementation of Budget Proposals 2016,
              subject to amendment being made to the Finance Act, Vehicle Entitlement Levy is introduced
              with effect from 01.01.2016 in lieu of "Motor Vehicle Importer Licence Fee" imposed under
              the Finance Act No.1 0 of2015.

              The Levy should be collected by banks from the importers of motor vehicles at opening of
              Letters of Credit (LC) for the importation of such vehicles as stated in the following Table.
