               -, Imposition of Economic Service Charge (ESC) in Advance on the turnover
                  of Importers of items subject to Special Commodity Levy

                   As instructed by the Ministry of Finance on implementation of Budget Proposals 2016,
                   subject to amendment being made to the Economic Service Charge Act, charging of ESC in
                   advance on the turnover of importers of items subject to Special Commodity Levy (SCL)
                   under the Special Commodity Levy Act, No. 48 of 2007, is introduced with effect from
                   01.04.2016.

                   The ESC in advance should be paid either in cash or bank draft to the banks using the pay-in-
                   slip available at the Inland Revenue by the importers on each consignment of importation of
                   items subject to SCL before clearance of such items from the Sri Lanka Customs, at the rate
                   of 0.5% on the CIF value certified by the Director General of Customs.

                   By producing the paid slip, the importer is able to get an "ESC Clearance Certificate" from
                   the Commissioner of Taxpayer Service Unit which should be produced to the Director
                   General of Customs.

                   In addition,               following   details also should be submitted                     III   order to get the clearance
                   certificate.

                         1.       Name of the importer
                         2.       Address
                         3.       TIN No.
                         4.       Income Tax file no. (If differs from the TIN No.)
                         5.       Customs Assessment No.
                         6.       Imported Item and amount of SCL paid
                         7.       Date of Importation
                         8.       CIF Value as per the Customs Assessment
                         9.       Amount of ESC paid and the date
