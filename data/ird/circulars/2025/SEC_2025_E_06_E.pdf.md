Risk Based Refund Scheme

1. Introduction

Following the repeal of the Simplified Value Added Tax (hereinafter referred to as the
“SVAT”) Scheme, the Inland Revenue Department (hereinafter referred to as the “IRD”)
shall implement a Risk-Based Refund Scheme (hereinafter referred to as the “RBRS”) for
Value Added Tax refunds (hereinafter referred to as “VAT refunds”). This circular is issued
to provide a brief overview of the RBRS and to outline important instructions for taxpayers
regarding the processing and issuance of VAT refunds under the RBRS. Registered persons
under the VAT Act are advised to carefully review the following guidelines to ensure
compliance with the requirements of the scheme.

2. Risk Based Refund Scheme

The RBRS is designed to streamline VAT refund process by assessing refund claims based
on the level of risk associated with any eligible exporter defined under section 83 of the
VAT Act. Additionally, any project approved by the Commissioner General of Inland
Revenue (hereinafter referred to as “CGIR”) under section 22(7) of the VAT Act or any
registered person whose value of supplies of goods or services was more than fifty per
centum of his total supplies for that taxable period who is supplying goods or services to
any Strategic Development Project identified under subsection (4) of section 3 of the
Strategic Development Project Act, No. 14 of 2008 or any specified project identified by
the Minister under item (ii) of paragraph (f) of Part II of the First Schedule to this Act or
item (xxi) of paragraph (b) of Part III of the First Schedule to this Act (hereinafter referred
to as the “Eligible Projects”) shall be eligible to receive VAT refunds under the RBRS.

In terms of Section 22(5) (f) (d) (ii) of the VAT Act, the law provides expeditious
processing of refunds not later than forty-five (45) days from the due date of VAT return
submission or from the date of compliance with Notice 2 issued by the IRD, whichever
falls later, for Eligible Exporters or Eligible Projects (hereinafter referred to as the “Eligible
Person”) categorized as low-risk, medium-risk, or high-risk. According to the “RBRS",
refund claims are processed subject to the risk assessment based on predefined criteria to
identify the likelihood of inaccuracy, non-compliance and overall behavior and reliability.
In general, eligible persons classified as low-risk or medium-risk will be issued refunds
without pre verification except the circumstances that officials think that pre verification is
necessary, while eligible person classified as high-risk shall be subject to pre verification
measures prior to the issuance of refunds.

As part of the RBRS, all approved refunds will be transferred directly to the Eligible
Person’s designated bank account. Eligible Persons are therefore required to ensure that
their bank account details registered with the tax authority are accurate and up to date. Any
discrepancies in the bank information may result in delays or failure in processing the
refund. It is the responsibility of the Eligible Persons to verify and update their banking
information through the prescribed portal.

3. Process of the Risk Based Refund Scheme
i. Identifying Eligible Persons

a) Eligible Exporter

According to Section 83 of the VAT Act “eligible exporter” means, a registered person
whose value of zero-rated supplies as defined under section 7 of this Act, during the
preceding calendar year was greater than fifty percent of the total value of supplies
made by that person during the same period.

b) Registered persons supplied to Eligible Projects — Registered person who made
supplies of goods or services, more than fifty per centum (50%) of the total supplies
to any Strategic Development Project or Special Project approved by the Minister

Any registered person whose value of supplies of goods or services was more than fifty
per centum of his total supplies for that taxable period who is supplying goods or
services to any Strategic Development Project identified under subsection (4) of section
3 of the Strategic Development Project Act, No. 14 of 2008 or any specified project
identified by the Minister under item (ii) of paragraph (f) of Part II of the First Schedule
to this Act or item (xxi) of paragraph (b) of Part III of the First Schedule to this Act
shall be eligible for the refund under RBRS

Furthermore, any registered person whose value of supplies of goods or services to any
Eligible Project, other than a project approved under Section 22(7) of the VAT Act,
exceeds fifty percent of the total value of supplies made during a taxable period, shall

Developing Risk Criteria and Risk Rating Categorization
The risk criteria will be developed based on the fallowing factors.

a) Taxpayer’s return and payment compliance history.

b) Audit history,

c) Consistency and stable revenue history.

d) Refund history,

e) Business reliability based on 3rd party data (eg. Custom, Central bank...),
f) Length of stay in the business.

g) Any other factors that CGIR thinks necessary.

This criteria will be used to identify the likelihood of accuracy, compliance, and to
assess Overall taxpayer behavior and reliability in order to categorize each Eligible
Person’s risk rating.

The eligible persons will be classified as low, medium, or high risk for the
administration of the RBRS. Such risk ratings shall be effective from the taxable period
commencing October 1, 2025, and remain valid until reviewed or revoked, with reviews
conducted at six-month intervals or as deemed necessary.

On behalf of the CGIR, the CIR-RMU will identify Eligible Exporters for the RBRS
based on the VAT return data of the calendar year 2024 for the initial eligibility period
from October 1, 2025, to March 31, 2026. Thereafter, eligibility will be reviewed
annually using VAT return data of the immediately preceding calendar year, effective
from April 1 to March 31 of the following year, or until cancellation.

Furthermore, the CIR-RMU, on behalf of the CGIR, will identify Eligible Projects—
excluding those approved under Section 22(7) of the VAT Act—for the purposes of the
RBRS. This identification shall be based on records available with the IRD, including
VAT return data. Eligibility for such projects shall be effective from the taxable period
commencing October 1, 2025, or any subsequent taxable period in which the project is
registered as a Strategic Development Project or a Special Project. This eligibility shall
remain in effect until the project’s expiry date or until cancellation, whichever occurs
earlier.

Recommended Actions to Maintain or Achieve Low Taxpayer Risk Rating

To ensure that eligible persons remain in the low-risk category, or transition from
medium/high risk to low risk, the following actions should be taken in line with the
assessed risk factors:
a) Taxpayer’s Return and Payment Compliance History

e File all tax returns accurately and on time.

e Ensure full and timely payment of all tax liabilities.

e Avoid late filings, underreporting, and non-compliance penalties.

e Respond promptly to any notices from the IRD.

b) Audit History
Maintain accurate and complete records to withstand audits.

Cooperate fully during audits and provide requested documentation.
e Avoid recurring audit adjustments by improving internal controls.
e Implement recommendations provided during past audits.

c) Consistency and Stability of Revenue History
e Ensure financial reporting reflects true and consistent revenue trends.
e Avoid large unexplained fluctuations in reported income.
e Use professional accounting services to maintain reliable records.

d) Refund History
e Only claim refunds when properly due and supported with documentation.
e Avoid excessive refund claims that could trigger suspicion.

e) Business Reliability Based on 3rd Party Data (e.g., Customs, Central Bank)
e Ensure consistency between tax returns and third-party data (e.g. import/export
data, banking information).
e Comply with customs, financial, and regulatory reporting obligations.
e Resolve any discrepancies flagged by other government agencies.

f) Length of Time in Business
e For newer businesses: Establish a strong compliance track record early.
e For established businesses: Maintain consistent and transparent operations over
time.
e Demonstrate long-term stability and business continuity.

Issuance of the Refund

The IRD has established two units namely “Export Refund Processing Unit” for the
issuance of refund and “Export Refund Verification Unit” for the pre-verification of
refunds to ensure smooth functioning of the refund process under RBRS,

Proper refund claims submitted by the eligible persons classified as low-risk or
medium-risk shall be automatically assigned to the Export Refund Processing Unit for
issuance of the refund, without being subjected to pre-verification.

Refund claims submitted by the eligible persons classified as high-risk shall be
automatically assigned to the Export Refund Verification Unit for pre-verification,
which must be completed prior to the issuance of the refund by the Export Refund
Processing Unit.

A field visit shall be conducted by designated officials of the IRD, and the
recommendation and approval of the relevant Senior Commissioner of the IRD shall be
required.

Refund process will be initiated based on the values declared in the VAT Return at the
on-return processing stage.

Every collectable taxes shall be paid or recovered from the VAT refund prior to the
issuance of the refund.

Where any material or significant risk-related information is received by the CGIR
during the relevant refund period, and in the opinion of the CGIR such verification is
necessary for the purpose of safeguarding government revenue, a refund claim may be
subjected to verification procedures.

The CGIR shall have the authority in the selection of refund claims for Post-Refund
Audit to ensure the accuracy, validity, and integrity of such refund claims and to
promote voluntary compliance and transparency in the administration of VAT.

4. Refund Rejection or Withheld

The CGIR, or any officer duly authorized by him for that purpose, may suspend or defer
the processing of a refund claim in any of the following circumstances, until such time as
the eligible person has complied with the requirements of the Act and discharged any
outstanding liability.

i. | Where the eligible person fails to respond to a notice issued under the provisions of
Section 21 of the VAT Act, for the purpose of obtaining full and complete information
in respect of a refund, within the time period specified in such notice.

## Page 9

ii. | Where a notice is issued by an officer indicating that there is reasonable suspicion—
supported by material facts or information available to the CGIR—that the refund
claim contains false, exaggerated, misleading, or otherwise inaccurate particulars or
representations.

ili. | Where the eligible person is in default of payment of any collectable tax, penalty, or
interest lawfully due and payable under the provisions of any Act administered by the
CGIR or any regulations made thereunder.

5. Reporting & Compliance Monitoring
The CGIR shall make necessary arrangements to monitor the process of RBRS to ensure
timely and efficient processing of claims.
