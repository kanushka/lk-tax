                   Dear Sir,



                   Furnishing of Tax clearance Certificate to Banks and Authorized Dealers

                   Please be informed that the circular issued on 06.02.2014 in respect of outward remittances
                   made by commercial banks and authorized dealers has been revised. Therefore, you are kindly
                   requested to comply with this circular with immediate effect.



                                                                                   PART A


                   A clearance certificate is not required in relation to following remittances.

                       (i)        Remittance of sale proceeds of quoted shares owned by non-residents in companies                         resident
                                  in Sri Lanka.

                       (ii)       Remittance   of dividend           paid to non-resident          shareholders    by resident   or non-resident
                                  companies.

                       (iii)      Foreign investments by residents of Sri Lanka.

                       (iv)       Transportation expenses in relation to freight forwarding,                       couner   services   and airline
                                  services involving the carriage of passengers and goods.

                       (v)        Payments to expatriate employees which include wages, salaries and other benefits which
                                  has been subjected to tax under the PAYE scheme.

(vi)     Remittances by export companies in respect of services in relation to advertising and
            marketing performed outside Sri Lanka.

   (vii)    Remittances by export companies in respect of registration of trademarks and other

   (viii)    Remittances in relation to annual subscription for membership of professional bodies,
            journals, magazines and other publication.


                                             PARTB

A clearance certificate    from the Commissioner             General of Inland Revenue             is
required for the following remittances

   (i)      Communication    services, which includes telecommunication   and internet services.

   (ii)     Computer software and information services.

   (iii)    Royalties, license and franchise fees.

   (iv)     Remittance in respect of foreign loans, capital repayment and interest payments, if
            such loan has been obtained prior to 01.04.2012.


   (v)      Remittances to foreign contractors engaged in public and private sector services in Sri
            Lanka which include design, construction and installation of projects,.

                Contract payments
                Surplus Funds arising from the contracts & projects.

   (vi)     Remittances in respect of services rendered by persons outside Sri Lanka in relation to
            management services.

   (vii)    Remittances on service contracts, which include Human Resources Management and
            Information Technology Management.


You are kindly requested to collect a copy of the Tax Clearance Certificate issued by the
Inland Revenue department in respect of items listed in PART B , as a condition of
approval of the above remittances in order to ensure that the remittances are made after
having complied with the tax laws.
